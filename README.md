# 📊 Sales Analysis and Amazon Products

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing **Amazon product sales data** to extract meaningful insights about sales trends, customer behavior, and product performance.

The goal is to use **Data Science and Data Analytics techniques** to support better business decisions and understand market patterns.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze sales trends over time  
- Identify top-performing products  
- Understand customer behavior  
- Provide data-driven insights for business growth
